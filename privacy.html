1. Who we are

Luniva ("Luniva", "we", "us") is a personal menstrual-health and wellness companion available at myluniva.netlify.app. This policy explains what information Luniva handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

To give you an account, we collect only what's needed to sign you in:

  • Name — so Luniva can greet you.
  • Email address — your login, and where we send verification, login and password-reset codes.
  • Password — stored by our login service in a protected (hashed) form. We never see it and it is never saved in your browser.
  • If you choose "Continue with Google" — Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with our login service. Luniva uses only your name and email.
  • Basic technical information — like any website, our hosting and login providers automatically receive your IP address, browser type and the time of your visits, which they use to deliver the service and keep it secure.

3. What stays on your device

Everything you track in Luniva is stored only in your browser's storage on the device you use, and is not sent to us or to anyone else. This includes:

  • Period dates and cycle settings
  • Daily check-ins: flow, pain level and location, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, cravings, optional weight and notes
  • Your height (if you add it), reminder choices and in-app notifications

Because these records live on your device, clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or switching devices means they won't be there. Use Settings → Export Data to keep a copy. If a different account signs in on the same device, Luniva asks before removing the previous account's records from that device.

4. How we use information

  • To create your account, verify your email and let you log in.
  • To send the emails you ask for: verification codes, login codes and password-reset codes. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To keep Luniva secure — for example, limiting repeated login attempts.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Luniva doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your health records on your device and to remember that you're logged in. Nothing is used to track you across other websites.

7. How long we keep it

  • Health records stay on your device until you delete them (Settings → Delete Data) or clear your browser data.
  • Account details are kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove it from our login service.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live — including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. In practice:

  • Get a copy: Settings → Export Data downloads everything Luniva stores on your device.
  • Correct: change your name and details in Settings.
  • Delete from this device: Settings → Delete Data removes all Luniva data from the device and logs you out.
  • Delete your account: email us from your account's email address and we'll delete it.
  • Questions or complaints: contact us (below). You can also contact your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

Connections to Luniva and our providers are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored in protected form by our login service, and access to account data is restricted. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked — anyone who can open your browser may be able to see the records stored there.

10. Age

Luniva is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you're under 18, please use Luniva only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account without that consent, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Not medical advice

Luniva helps you record and understand your personal patterns. It does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice. If something feels unusual or keeps happening, please talk to a healthcare professional.

12. Changes to this policy

If Luniva starts handling information differently — for example, if we add an option to sync your records to your account — we'll update this policy and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before the change affects you.
