Getting started

Set up Luniva in 5 minutes

Create your account, put Luniva on your home screen like an app, and get one gentle reminder a day. Free, no ads, and your health records stay on your phone.

The steps

  1. Create your account
  2. Add Luniva to your home screen
  3. Turn on daily reminders
  4. Use it on two devices (optional)
  5. If something doesn't work

1 Create your account

  1. Open myluniva.netlify.app and tap Start Your Journey.
  2. Sign up with your email and a password — or tap Continue with Google.
  3. If you used email, type the 6-digit code we email you. (Can't see it? Check your spam or promotions folder.)
  4. Answer four quick questions: your name, your usual cycle, what you'd like to track and which reminders you want.

Your periods, check-ins and notes are saved on your phone, not on our servers.

2 Add Luniva to your home screen

Luniva works like an app: it gets its own icon, opens full screen, works offline and can send reminders. Nothing to download from an app store.

Install Luniva on this device

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

  1. Open myluniva.netlify.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install button on the Luniva page if you see one — or tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install to confirm. The Luniva icon appears on your home screen.
  1. Open myluniva.netlify.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom or top of the screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, open Luniva from its home-screen icon — that's needed for reminders on iPhone.
  1. Open myluniva.netlify.app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar — or the ⋮ / … menu → Install Luniva.
  3. Luniva opens in its own window and appears with your other apps.

3 Turn on daily reminders

  1. Open Luniva and go to Settings (tap your initial at the top, then Settings).
  2. In Notifications, switch on the reminders you'd like — period, pad, daily check-in, motivation…
  3. Scroll to 📲 Phone reminders, choose a time in Remind me at and tap Turn on phone reminders.
  4. Tap Allow when your phone asks about notifications.
  5. Tap Send a test — you should see “Reminders are working 🎉” within a few seconds.

Period reminders are discreet by default — they just say “A gentle reminder from Luniva”. Turn on Show period details in reminders if you'd like more. What a reminder says is worked out on your phone; your health data is never sent.

On iPhone and iPad, reminders need iOS 16.4 or newer and Luniva opened from its home-screen icon (step 2).

4 Use it on two devices optional

Records live on each device. To have them on your phone and another device — or to keep a backup — go to Settings → Encrypted sync & backup and choose a passphrase. Your records are locked with it before they leave your phone, so nobody else (not even Luniva) can read them. Use the same passphrase on your other device.

Write your passphrase down somewhere safe. If it's lost, the synced copy can't be opened — only deleted and started again.

? If something doesn't work

I didn't get the email code

Wait a minute and check your spam, junk or promotions folder. Still nothing? Go back and tap Send a new code, and make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

I don't see an Install button

Use the browser menu instead (Android: ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari). If Luniva is already installed, the button is hidden — look for the icon on your home screen.

I don't see “Phone reminders” in Settings, or it says to add Luniva to the Home Screen

On iPhone, open Luniva from its home-screen icon, not from Safari. Elsewhere, use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Samsung Internet.

It says notifications are blocked

Android: long-press the Luniva icon → App info → Notifications → allow. Or in Chrome: ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Notifications → allow myluniva.netlify.app.
iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Luniva → Allow Notifications.
Then come back to Luniva → Settings and turn phone reminders on again.

The test worked but the daily reminder didn't arrive

Check that Do Not Disturb or Focus isn't on at that time, and that battery saver isn't stopping Chrome or Luniva. Reminders arrive within a few minutes after the hour you chose, once a day.

I got a new phone

Log in on the new phone. If you turned on encrypted sync, enter your passphrase and your records come back. Then turn on phone reminders again on the new phone.
