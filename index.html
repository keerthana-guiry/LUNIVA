About Luniva

What is Luniva?

Luniva is a personal menstrual-health and wellness companion that runs in your web browser. It helps people who menstruate record their periods and daily wellbeing, understand their own patterns, prepare for their next period, and find a little comfort on harder days.

Luniva is free to use. It is a tracking and wellness tool — it does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.

With Luniva you can

  • Log period start and end dates on a calendar
  • Record blood flow, pain, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep and cravings
  • See your average cycle length and personal patterns
  • Get a prediction of your next period and a reminder to carry a pad
  • Open the Companion for kind words, jokes and small distractions

Your account and Google sign-in

You can create a Luniva account with your email address, or choose Continue with Google. If you use Google, Luniva receives only your name, email address and profile picture, and uses your name and email solely to create your account and sign you in. Your health records stay on your own device and are never shared with Google or anyone else.

How It Works

Track → Understand → Prepare → Support

Four gentle steps that turn everyday notes into care for yourself.

  1. 1. Track

    Log your period, flow, pain, sleep, mood and cravings in seconds.

  2. 2. Understand

    See your averages and patterns, calculated from your own records.

  3. 3. Prepare

    Know when your next period may start and when to carry a pad.

  4. 4. Support

    Find encouragement, a joke or a tiny distraction on harder days.

Privacy

Your health data belongs to you.

Luniva is built so that the most personal details of your life stay under your control.

Personal tracking

Luniva is for you alone — there is no feed and nothing to share.

User-controlled information

Export everything or delete it all from Settings, any time.

Minimal data collection

Only what you choose to track. Weight is off unless you turn it on.

Private experience

No ads, no trackers, no selling of your information.

Your health records are stored locally in your browser and never leave your device. Only your sign-in details (your email and a securely protected password) are kept by our login service. Read our Privacy Policy.
